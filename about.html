Our History

A Legacy of Woodworking Excellence

Founded in 1995, ReviveWood began as a humble family workshop dedicated to saving discarded furniture. Over the decades, our passion for breathing life into aged wood has grown into a premier restoration service trusted by antique collectors, interior designers, and families wanting to preserve their heirlooms.

We believe that every scratch, dent, and faded stain tells a story. Our job is not to erase history, but to honor it by meticulously restoring the structural integrity and aesthetic beauty of each piece, ensuring it lasts for generations to come.

Our Mission

To provide unparalleled furniture restoration services combining traditional artistry with modern sustainability, preserving the soul of every piece.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in luxury furniture restoration, recognized globally for our uncompromising quality and ethical craftsmanship.

Traditional Techniques

French Polishing

A laborious process of applying shellac by hand to create a high-gloss, deep finish that highlights the wood grain beautifully.

Veneer Repair

Meticulous patching of chipped or peeling veneer, using period-accurate glues and perfectly matched wood species.

Joinery Restoration

Dismantling and re-gluing loose joints using traditional hide glues for strength without compromising future reversibility.

Our Quality Standards

  • Authenticity: We use materials and finishes appropriate to the period of the piece.
  • Durability: Repairs are engineered to withstand daily use while looking seamless.
  • Sustainability: We prioritize eco-friendly strippers and finishes whenever possible.
  • Transparency: No hidden costs; detailed condition reports provided upon request.
